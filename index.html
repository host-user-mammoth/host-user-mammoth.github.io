How to read the board
Checkmark = best price across FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM, Caesars. The Sharp-tagged rows (Pinnacle, Novig, ProphetX, LowVig.ag, BetOnline.ag — whichever have posted) are the reference line every pick is priced against — benchmarks, not books to bet at.

Odds are American format: −150 means bet $150 to win $100; +150 means a $100 bet wins $150. Smaller negative / bigger positive is always the better price.
